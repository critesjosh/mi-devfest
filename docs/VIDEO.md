# Video demo

[![ShiftProof demo video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/2-2k48i3ESQ/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://youtu.be/2-2k48i3ESQ)

**Watch: https://youtu.be/2-2k48i3ESQ** (1 minute)

ShiftProof helps experienced machinists turn what the written work instruction leaves out into reviewed guidance the next shift can use. The video runs the three steps: Compare, Review and Ask.

Recorded against the live app with IBM Granite on watsonx.ai (`ibm/granite-4-h-small`). All documents shown are fictional training fixtures, and the veteran is not a real person. The voiceover (Gemini TTS) and the background music (Lyria) are AI-generated; see the AI-tools list in [`shiftproof/README.md`](../shiftproof/README.md).
