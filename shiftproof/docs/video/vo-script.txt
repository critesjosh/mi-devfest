# ShiftProof demo video: voiceover script (final, v1)
# Written by Claude Opus 5.5. Only Anthropic models or teammates change these words (COPY.md).
# Gemini TTS reads the spoken lines exactly. Lines starting with '#' are never sent.
# Format: [beat @ m:ss] then the spoken text. The cue is when the line starts in the video.
# Spoken forms: "one-fourteen" is WI-114; "two thousandths" is 0.002 in. Captions use the written forms (captions.srt).

[user @ 0:00.3]
An apprentice's first bracket reads two thousandths over. Work instruction one-fourteen, Rev C, says to adjust the wear offset.

[problem @ 0:09.7]
The veteran's notes say: wait. A warm part reads long, and chasing the offset makes every part after it short. The instruction doesn't say that.

[compare @ 0:20]
ShiftProof puts the two side by side. IBM Granite flags what the instruction leaves out, and quotes both sources. The app checks every quote.

[review @ 0:30]
Granite asks the veteran a follow-up, then drafts a guide card from the answer. The veteran confirms it. The quality lead approves it.

[ask @ 0:40.8]
Back at the machine, the apprentice asks. The answer uses only approved cards, and cites the veteran's words.

[refuse @ 0:49.5]
No card covers it? Ask your lead or quality.

[impact @ 0:53.5]
What the veteran knows, reviewed, at the machine. ShiftProof, on IBM Granite.
