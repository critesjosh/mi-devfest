# WI-114 Rev C: Op 20, mill pocket on mounting bracket

**Part:** BRK-2207 mounting bracket, 6061-T6 aluminum, 4.000 in long
**Machine:** 3-axis vertical mill, 6 in vise
**Critical dimension:** pocket length 2.500 in +/- 0.001 in

## Steps

1. Load program O2207 and verify the tool list against the setup sheet.
2. Clamp the part in the vise against the fixed jaw, on 1/2 in parallels.
3. Indicate the fixed jaw. It must be within 0.0005 in over its length.
4. Set work offset G54 from the left end of the part and the top of the part.
5. Run the first part.
6. Measure the pocket length with a 2-3 in micrometer. Record it on the first-article sheet.
7. If the pocket is out of tolerance, adjust the tool wear offset and rerun.
8. When the first part is approved by quality, run production.
